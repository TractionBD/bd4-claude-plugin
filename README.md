# BD4 for Claude

Work your BD4 relationships from Claude: see who to reach out to today, get an
outreach brief, draft a message in your own voice, and log what happened.

You need a BD4 account at [app.bd4pros.com](https://app.bd4pros.com).

## Install

**Claude (web, desktop or mobile):** open **Customize**, choose
**Add > Add marketplace**, and enter `TractionBD/bd4-claude-plugin`. Then
install **bd4** and sign in to BD4 when asked.

**Claude Code:**

```
/plugin marketplace add TractionBD/bd4-claude-plugin
/plugin install bd4@bd4
```

## What you get

- `/bd4:today`: who BD4 suggests you reach out to today
- `/bd4:brief <name>`: the outreach brief for a person
- `/bd4:log <who and what happened>`: log a call, meeting or note

Claude also follows BD4's playbooks for your daily list, next best actions,
outreach briefs, drafting in your voice, logging interactions, reminders and
your hotlist. It always asks before it sends an email or changes a contact.
